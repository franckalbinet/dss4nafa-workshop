# Session 3: OREFA, optimizing decision making

Speaker: Floris Abrams. 30 minutes.

OREFA plans the remediation of farmland contaminated with caesium-137: where to remediate, how and when, with the farm parcel as the unit of decision. It comes from the PhD research of Floris Abrams at KU Leuven and SCK CEN (2023), with case studies in the Maarkebeek catchment in Belgium and the Niida catchment near Fukushima. The thesis is on [Lirias](https://lirias.kuleuven.be/handle/20.500.12942/730653).
