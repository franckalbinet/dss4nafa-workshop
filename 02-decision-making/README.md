# Session 2: DSS4NAFA decision making

Speaker: Franck Albinet. 30 minutes.

How the DSS4NAFA dashboards turn laboratory results into restriction options, and how [trufl](https://fr.anckalbi.net/trufl/) allocates each round of samples to make restriction decisions right. The open questions from this work are in [open-questions.md](../open-questions.md).

The [dashboards](dashboards/) folder has step-by-step screenshots of three dashboards:

- `hotspots-dashboard.pdf`: the hotspot map of radioactivity levels by foodstuff, radionuclide and time.
- `logmap-dashboard.pdf`: the samples planned, collected and analysed over time.
- `restriction-dashboard.pdf`: the food restriction dashboard.
